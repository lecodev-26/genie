🧞 Genie demo

Think of an animal, character, object, place, film, game, book, or another recognizable thing.

  1. Think of it without telling Genie.
  2. Say listo when you are ready.
  3. Answer the adaptive questions with yes, no, or don't know.
  4. Genie narrows the possibilities and makes a guess.
